Featured Expeditions

Salmon River Canyon

A classic full-day journey traversing dramatic basalt gorges. Ideal for mixed-level groups looking for steady action and breathtaking canyon panoramas.

  • Difficulty: Class III - IV
  • Duration: Full Day (6 hours)
  • Minimum Age: 10+

Thunder Gorge Extreme

Our most challenging technical route with continuous Class IV and V drops. Requires physical stamina, strong paddling, and prior white water experience.

  • Difficulty: Class IV - V
  • Duration: 4 Hours
  • Minimum Age: 16+

Pioneer Scenic Float

A serene wilderness journey with mild splash spots, scenic wildlife viewing, and peaceful swimming pools. The perfect choice for beginners and families.

  • Difficulty: Class I - II
  • Duration: Half Day (3 hours)
  • Minimum Age: 6+
